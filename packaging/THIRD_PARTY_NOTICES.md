# Third-party software shipped with ProbeDesk

ProbeDesk's installers include the following components. Their licence texts are
available from the projects listed here.

- **probe-rs** (command line tools) - dual-licensed under the MIT and Apache-2.0
  licences. https://github.com/probe-rs/probe-rs
- **Chip descriptions for Geehy APM32 devices** (`resources/chips`) - generated with
  probe-rs `target-gen` from Geehy's CMSIS-Packs.
  https://www.keil.arm.com/packs/
- **Qt 6** - used under the GNU LGPL v3 and dynamically linked. https://www.qt.io
